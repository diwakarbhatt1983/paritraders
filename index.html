Our story

A shop built on repeat customers

Pari Traders started as a single building-material counter in 1998. Today we supply homeowners, contractors and hardware shops across the district.

Why we exist

Honest grading, open prices

Quality in this trade usually means whatever the seller says it means. We built the shop around the opposite idea: three clearly defined tiers, the same price list for everyone, and a bill that matches what was promised.

That approach costs us a little on each sale and earns us customers who come back for a decade. Most of our business now comes from referrals and standing monthly orders.

“If a customer has to ask twice whether they were treated fairly, we have already lost them.” — Founding principle, Pari Traders
Milestones

How we grew

1998 One counter, one family

Opened as a small building-material counter in the old bazaar with cement and bricks.

2006 Paints and steel added

Expanded into paints, TMT steel and plumbing pipes for local builders and families.

2014 Wholesale desk

Started supplying contractors and hardware shops with monthly standing orders.

2024 Three-tier catalogue

Standardised every product line into High, Medium and Low quality with published rates.

Fair grading

Open pricing

Homeowners and contractors are quoted from the same list. No surprise line items.

Dependable supply

Standing orders are confirmed weekly so sites are not left short mid-construction.
